SIMPLER WATERFALL DIRECTIONS K1-K6

Use case: logo-brand.
Generate ONE landscape logo concept board with SIX radically different MINIMAL waterfall logo ideas for Imladris, a private personal media library whose name evokes Rivendell.
CRITICAL ART DIRECTION: previous iterations all repeated a scenic waterfall between two craggy rocks above an oval pool. The user explicitly rejects that lack of variety and wants substantially less complexity. This board must explore six genuinely different graphic constructions. Do NOT draw six versions of the same landscape. Design emblematic SYMBOLS, not tiny landscape illustrations. Each logo should be drawable as roughly 2-4 simple vector paths. Thick forms, very few Bézier curves, wide open negative spaces. No cracks, interior water hairlines, jagged cliff textures, stacked pool ripples, decorative detail. One flat black ink on pure white. No shading, gradients, mockup objects, shadows, texture or 3D.
Layout: clean editorial comparison board, 3 columns x 2 rows, large equal cells with generous whitespace. Small header "Imladris", subtitle "Simpler waterfall directions". Every cell one large logo with its short exact ID and name in modest sans-serif below.
TOP LEFT "K1 · Ribbon": a single broad sculptural water ribbon folding over a horizontal ledge and descending, one flowing black shape with an open white inner curve, only a short ledge implied. OPEN free silhouette, no framing cliffs, no pool. Graceful, asymmetric, typographic level of reduction.
TOP MIDDLE "K2 · Gorge": two nearly rectangular solid black masses of offset height create a broad waterfall of WHITE negative space between them; their inner edges form one simple curved water lip and falling slot. Compact geometric square silhouette, only two black shapes, no bottom ellipse or water stripe. Very architectural.
TOP RIGHT "K3 · Round fall": compact solid black CIRCULAR disc with one broad white waterfall cleft cut from upper edge downward, curving at its base into a small basin that stays inside disc. One dark silhouette, one negative cut. No exterior cliffs or pool. Rounded seal-like silhouette without border rings.
BOTTOM LEFT "K4 · Cascade": THREE short bold horizontal ledge bars stepping downward, linked by one broad falling ribbon. An abstract rhythmic staircase of falling water, angular geometric construction, far more horizontal, no rocks or circles. Immediately distinguishable silhouette from other cells.
BOTTOM MIDDLE "K5 · Edge": SIDE-PROFILE waterfall pouring over a single simple black cliff ledge on the left into open white space on right. One dark cliff shape plus one bold gracefully downward-curving water stroke, no second cliff, no basin. Wide asymmetric emblem, economy of strokes.
BOTTOM RIGHT "K6 · Veil": two broad smooth bowed black forms make a softly domed silhouette with a central WHITE waterfall ribbon widening toward the bottom and exiting openly. Water is the central negative shape. No mountains, no rock details, no pool. Organic arch-like silhouette radically different from rectilinear K2.
Make all six designs look like serious minimal identity marks with graphic craft and clearly different outer silhouettes. Keep the subject waterfall, avoid river roads, arrows, sound equalizers, generic mountain sun logos, trees, houses, books, stars, initials, runes. NO extra cutouts or ornament to make them "more beautiful". Emphasize recognizability at small size. Labels verbatim.

SIMPLER REFUGE DIRECTIONS L1-L6

Use case: logo-brand.
Generate ONE landscape comparison board of SIX radically different MINIMAL shelter-and-waterfall logo concepts for Imladris, a private personal media library named after Rivendell.
The owner retained some earlier house/falls illustrations but said ALL variations looked basically identical and too complex. This is a fresh design exploration, not tweaking the same steep-gabled chapel on craggy rocks. Change the WHOLE graphic construction, composition and outside silhouette between designs. Interpret Rivendell as graceful shelter + flowing water, reduce each to roughly 2-4 easy vector paths. No scenes. No cliff cracks, water hairlines, windows, roof trim, pool ripples or fancy architectural details. Very thick strokes where used, broad open spaces. Black on pure white, crisp flat vector-like graphic design, no gradients, texture, shadows or 3D.
Layout: landscape board, 3 columns x 2 rows, generous margins and consistent optical mark sizes. Modest header "Imladris", subtitle "Simpler refuge directions". Exact small sans-serif label under each emblem. Marks must have emphatically different silhouettes.
TOP LEFT "L1 · Roof + ribbon": ONLY one solid asymmetric gently peaked roof canopy hovering over one bold descending waterfall ribbon. No walls, door, rocks or pool. The roof slope is graceful and elven, but not a pagoda. Two shapes, compact airy silhouette.
TOP MIDDLE "L2 · Door into water": ONE solid tall broad pointed-arch doorway block, its large open white entrance flows seamlessly downward into a wide waterfall opening and exits at the bottom. The water and doorway are the SAME negative space. No separate house sitting on a cliff, no roof ornament. One strong graphic silhouette, 1-2 shapes.
TOP RIGHT "L3 · Round refuge": solid ROUND dark medallion with a simple small peaked shelter silhouette and broad curved waterfall carved into it in WHITE negative space. Only one roof angle and one curve. No separate rock scenery, no border ring, no tiny architectural details. Circular silhouette, compact.
BOTTOM LEFT "L4 · One flowing line": bold MONOLINE open emblem where one continuous thick line rises into a modest asymmetric peaked roof, descends as the side of a shelter, then sweeps into a falling water curve. Open contour with generous white center. No solid cliff blocks. The line itself carries both roof and flow; 1 path, no ornament. Radically different open stroke construction.
BOTTOM MIDDLE "L5 · Over the edge": low WIDE horizontally cantilevered shelter roof resting on one short support over a broad single descending water ribbon at its far side. Side-elevation abstract Rivendell terrace, not centered house above water. 3 simple black shapes, no rocks, windows, pool or stairs. Wide directional silhouette.
BOTTOM RIGHT "L6 · Arched haven": one broad softly curved ARCH shelter shape with a subtly peaked crown embracing a single smooth vertical waterfall ribbon in its opening, open at base. Organic embracing silhouette, few curves, not a chapel or pagoda. At most 2 solid shapes. Architecture suggested by the shelter form, not drawn literally.
Art direction: emphasize invention and meaningful differences: canopy, fused doorway, circular negative space, open monoline, horizontal cantilever, organic arch. Do NOT homogenize these into six steep roofs sitting on identical cliffs. Spacious, balanced, literary character with restrained elven elegance. The house-and-water idea should be apparent without depicting a detailed house. Avoid gothic church spires, pagoda roofs, tiny windows, duplicated rooflines, rocks, pool ellipses, runes, fantasy ornament, branches, books, stars, initials, badges, shields and mockup objects. Labels verbatim.
