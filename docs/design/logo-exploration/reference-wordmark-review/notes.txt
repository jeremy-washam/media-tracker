Imladris wordmark references, round two

The owner requested another set of options before changing the app wordmark.
The reference round was produced while the app used Cormorant Garamond 600.
The owner subsequently selected H, Fondamento 400. The app now uses Fondamento
with the retained compact J2; the baseline and review screenshots remain history.

Style reference: user-provided screenshot, saved unchanged as reference.png.
The six NEW candidates are separate from A-F in the earlier comparison:
G: Almendra 400 — irregular medieval serif.
H: Fondamento 400 — calligraphic strokes and angled terminals.
I: Metamorphous 400 — broad carved fantasy forms.
J: IM Fell English 400 — antique printed lettering.
K: MedievalSharp 400 — angular handwritten lettering.
L: Uncial Antiqua 400 — rounded manuscript lettering.

All are actual fonts rendered in Chromium, not image-generated approximations.
Fonts, original licenses, source URLs, and SHA-256 hashes are in fonts/.
The current-font baseline uses the app's 600-weight name-only WOFF2, copied
without modification; its original license is current-cormorant-OFL.txt.

preview.html works offline and includes the supplied reference, the current
font baseline, large lockups, light/dark sidebar samples, an appearance toggle,
and a wordmark-size slider. Sidebar labels retain system typography. Wordmark
sizes in the small samples are 20 px, except 18 px Metamorphous and 17 px Uncial
Antiqua for optical balance. The symbol remains 30 px in every small sample.

Verification: all seven font faces (six candidates and one baseline) loaded;
no clipped lockups at the default size; appearance and size controls worked.
comparison-light.png and comparison-dark.png were captured after fonts loaded.
Selected replacement: H, Fondamento 400.

Final direction: the owner removed the wordmark. The app uses compact J2 alone;
no brand font is bundled or loaded. Prior comparisons remain as history.
