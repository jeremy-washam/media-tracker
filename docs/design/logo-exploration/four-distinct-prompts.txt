A — Wide solid cliffside silhouette

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE logo concept for Imladris: a private personal media library inspired by Rivendell. Not a comparison board. No text.
Art direction A: WIDE SOLID CLIFFSIDE SILHOUETTE. A long, low, inhabited elven HOUSE stretches horizontally along a terrace at the LEFT. Its architecture has a sweeping asymmetrical roof, two plainly rectangular open window cuts and one wide doorway: residential, NOT a pointed chapel. On the RIGHT, a waterfall falls off the terrace into open space. The house roof, terrace and single supporting cliff form a cohesive bold silhouette. The water is a broad descending shape or negative space on the right, with a straight bottom, no curled ribbon flourish. Asymmetric horizontal composition with the house occupying most of the width and water at the end. A clear physical relationship between house and waterfall.
Rendering: flat solid black silhouette on clean white, logo-level reduction, thick geometric/softly curved mass, economical shape construction, maximum 4-5 major shapes, no hairline detail. No pools, ripples, cracks, vines, trees, birds, stars, ornate gables, arch doorway, domed paired cliffs, S-ribbon symbols, badge frames, gradients, texture, shadow, or 3D. The house must read as a home, not merely a chevron hovering above water.
Composition: a single large centered standalone symbol with generous whitespace on a square image. No lettering, labels, promotional mockups or border. The shape should be about twice as wide as tall, with a strong horizontal roof. This is one of four visually unrelated logo directions; make this distinctly a solid landscape silhouette.

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE standalone logo concept for Imladris, a private personal media library whose name evokes Rivendell as a protected home of stories and knowledge. No text, no comparison sheet.
Art direction C: CIRCULAR NEGATIVE-SPACE VILLAGE SEAL. A bold solid BLACK DISC contains a quiet miniature composition cut out in WHITE: TWO connected small residential houses with slanted roofs perch on the upper left of a broad curving hillside. On the right, one broad WHITE WATERFALL falls from a high horizontal ledge, curves slightly as it drops, then connects to a white horizontal water opening along the bottom of the disc. The outer silhouette MUST be a clean true circle. The houses and waterfall MUST both be plainly recognizable, not merely a chevron and a ribbon. Visual language: bold pictographic linocut-like composition WITHOUT texture or hatch marks; sculptural WHITE cutout shapes in one solid BLACK circular field. Strong positive-negative balance, broad house masses and broad water gap. Circle evokes a private refuge seen through an opening. House placement upper-left and water descending on right, NOT a single pointed house centered above a cascade, NOT outline architecture. Use fewer than 6 major white cutouts, no scenery rendering, no individual rock marks, no pool ellipse, no trees, no stars, no roofline ornaments, no window rows.
Flat pure black and pure white, mathematically clean logo edges, no gray, gradient, shadow, texture, 3D, border rings, lettering, initials, labels or objects. Single large centered compact round symbol with generous whitespace on a square white canvas. Clear enough for a compact app icon.
