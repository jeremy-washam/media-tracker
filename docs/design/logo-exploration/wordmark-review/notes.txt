Imladris wordmark review

Uses the owner-preferred compact J2, refined as imladris-j2-compact-v2.svg.
Initially selected: A, Cormorant Garamond 600. The owner later selected
H, Fondamento 400, from the second reference set; that is now implemented.

Candidates
A: Cormorant Garamond 600 — expressive literary serif.
B: Instrument Serif 400 — narrow editorial serif.
C: Fraunces 500 — rounded serif, with SOFT 35, WONK 1, and optical size 48.
D: Alegreya 500 — sturdy literary serif.
E: Cinzel 500 — engraved Roman capitals/small capitals.
F: Manrope 500 — modern sans serif.

preview.html is standalone and works offline with six locally bundled fonts.
The top controls switch preview appearance and change large wordmark size.
Small sidebar samples show both appearances and use a 30 px symbol. Type is
optically adjusted in those samples: 20 px for most, 23 px Instrument Serif,
and 17 px Cinzel. Body/navigation labels retain the platform system font.

Font sources and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in fonts/sources.json. Original
OFL licenses are stored beside each unmodified TTF. The application now bundles a
name-only WOFF2 subset of Fondamento at weight 400 with its license.
Review HTML and screenshots retain the initial comparison as history.

Verification: all six FontFace objects loaded in Chromium from local files;
no clipping in logo or sidebar samples; appearance toggle and size control
work. comparison-light.png and comparison-dark.png were rendered only after
document.fonts.ready. The refined SVG parsed without errors and contains a
native path, not an embedded raster image.

Final direction: the owner removed the wordmark. The app uses compact J2 alone;
no brand font is bundled or loaded. Prior comparisons remain as history.
